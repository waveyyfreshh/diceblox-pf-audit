The Anatomy of the Exploit

This open-source toolkit exists to visually and mathematically prove the existence of a hardcoded 22% house override inside the Diceblox Blackjack algorithm. We utilize the exact Javascript engine (Chance.js) and the exact source code published by Diceblox to ensure zero bias.

The Golden Rule of Provably Fair: A fair Random Number Generator (RNG) is completely useless if the game's rules intercept the random number and force a rigged outcome anyway.

1. The VRF Misdirection (Cryptography vs. Logic)

When confronted with allegations of rigged code, casino operators often deploy complex cryptographic terms like Verifiable Random Functions (VRFs), Public Keys, and Nonces to confuse players and sound authoritative. This is a deliberate misdirection.

A legitimate Provably Fair system operates in two distinct steps:

  1. Step 1: Cryptography. The system uses cryptographic ingredients (Public Key, Client Seed, Nonce, Proof) to do complex math. This generates a single, secure, verifiable string of text called a Random Hash.
  2. Step 2: Game Logic. The casino's game code takes that single Random Hash, feeds it into a deck generator, and draws your cards.

Diceblox successfully passes Step 1. Their cryptography is secure. However, they entirely fail Step 2. They generate a perfectly fair Hash, but their Blackjack code actively intercepts the Hash to force the dealer to win.

2. Why does our Verifier only need 1 Input (The Hash)?

Diceblox's own fairness popup requires 5 parameters (Public Key, Parameters, Proof, etc.). Players often ask why our verification tool only requires the Random Hash. Are we skipping steps?

No. We are auditing the Game Logic, not the Cryptography. Once the cryptography creates the Hash, the game discards the other ingredients. The Javascript card-drawing engine only accepts the Random Hash as an input.

By inputting the final Hash into our Verifier tab, we inject the data precisely where the Blackjack game takes over. The systems are fully bidirectional: Hashes generated in our simulator work on Diceblox's tool, and hashes from Diceblox work perfectly on our tool.

3. The 22% Hardcoded Override

Below is the exact code extracted from Diceblox. It takes the Random Hash, rolls a 100-sided die (the "ticket"), and exactly 22% of the time, overrides the fair draw to artificially inject a 19, 20, or Blackjack directly into the Dealer's hand.
